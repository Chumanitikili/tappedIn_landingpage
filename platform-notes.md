# Approved Platform Messaging Notes

Source reviewed: https://rather.chat/?page_id=4534 on 26 August 2026.

Connect Pro is the commercial solution that connects mall visitors to WhatsApp after they register for Wi-Fi. TappedIn is the customer-facing name intended to appear in WhatsApp chats.

The supplied page describes the customer journey as: shopper arrives, connects to Wi-Fi, registers for free access, is connected on WhatsApp, receives personalised location-based offers, and visits stores or redeems offers.

Use these supported customer-oriented messages in the landing page:

- Deals, vouchers, coupons, and relevant updates can be delivered through WhatsApp.
- Offers can be targeted by location, time, and context.
- Redemption is instant and seamless through WhatsApp.
- The experience reaches people at a moment of high purchase intent.

Avoid treating published commercial metrics as universal TappedIn claims in the customer-facing page. The landing page should use clear descriptive copy rather than unaudited numerical performance promises.

## QR-Code Access Update

TappedIn can also be entered from a QR code placed wherever customers are, including venue touchpoints, posters, tables, or retail displays. Scanning directs the customer to the TappedIn WhatsApp channel, where they can access mall-wide deals as well as individual retailer vouchers, coupons, and discounts.

The page’s campaign imagery should visibly represent multiracial friends and families in natural shared shopping moments. The phone visual should retain a believable tall device ratio with comfortably scaled chat content at desktop and mobile sizes.
