# TappedIn Landing Page — Design Direction

## Three visual approaches

### Approach 1
**Theme Name:** Pocket Confetti

**Very Brief Intro:** A bright editorial celebration of small wins, using voucher-inspired edges, sticker-like annotations, and confident white space. It makes everyday saving feel useful, quick, and pleasingly tangible.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Approach 2
**Theme Name:** Message Momentum

**Very Brief Intro:** A crisp digital-service aesthetic that treats WhatsApp as the familiar place where value arrives. Rounded conversation surfaces and a scrolling rail make discovery feel natural rather than technical.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Approach 3
**Theme Name:** Good Things, Tapped

**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, modern retail language combining soft cyan space, electric blue containers, and tactile deal cards. It translates a simple chat interaction into a small moment of anticipation.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach — Good Things, Tapped

### Design Movement
The page draws on **contemporary editorial retail design**, where oversized type, paper-like layers, and expressive cutouts create a sense of immediacy without clutter.

### Core Principles
1. Make the path from message to saving visually obvious at every scroll depth.
2. Use roomy, editorial compositions rather than a dense conventional product grid.
3. Let the blue, turquoise, and green brand system signal optimism, trust, and action.
4. Keep every interaction familiar, approachable, and rooted in the language of a chat.

### Color Philosophy
Light cyan provides an open, low-friction canvas that evokes a fresh conversation. Vivid blue is reserved for high-attention product surfaces and primary calls to action, while green marks confirmation, saved value, and action completed. Dark Azure grounds the system so that it remains highly legible and commercially credible.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is organized as a **guided scroll narrative**: a generous hero with a conversation motif; a slim proof strip; a blue feature field that visually contains the WhatsApp experience; an off-axis process story; and a final contact conversion point. The rhythm uses staggered blocks and horizontal deal rails instead of stacking uniform centered cards.

### Signature Elements
1. Oversized rounded message bubbles with small timestamp and signal details.
2. Ticket-like deal cards with notched corners and slim dashed separators.
3. TappedIn’s check-and-tap icon appearing as a confirmation cue and visual marker.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel as clear as tapping a message: buttons have fast, subtle compression; deal cards lift lightly on hover; and navigation anchors scroll directly to the relevant story moment. The page should never behave like a dashboard or require the visitor to decipher controls.

### Animation
In motion-capable environments, hero chips rise into place in 180–260 ms with a gentle stagger. The chat bubble uses a single restrained upward reveal, while deal cards shift upward by two pixels on hover. Use the custom easing `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` and respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling nonessential entrances.

### Typography System
Headlines use **Vag Rounded Bold** where available, falling back to `Arial Rounded MT Bold` and rounded system sans-serif. Body content uses **Vag Rounded Regular** where available, falling back to `Nunito Sans`. Headlines are dense, low-line-height, and sentence case; body copy is calm and generously spaced for quick scanning.

### Brand Essence
**TappedIn helps people claim worthwhile deals in the WhatsApp chats they already use.**

Personality: **friendly, decisive, rewarding**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise and energising; calls to action sound like an invitation to receive something useful, not a technical request. Microcopy explains the value in plain language and avoids jargon.

Example lines:

> A better deal is one message away.

> Let value find you where you already chat.

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied TappedIn wordmark prominently in the header and use the supplied check-and-tap symbol as a visible brand marker in deal confirmations, feature callouts, and favicon treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
**TappedIn Turquoise — #38CAB5** is the owning colour: clear, energetic, and recognisably at home with the TappedIn check-and-tap gesture.

## Style Decisions

- TappedIn Turquoise `#38CAB5` is the primary action and deal-reveal colour; green is reserved for a saved, confirmed, or completed action.
- Each main story block includes a visible chat or deal artifact, such as a message bubble, notched voucher, timestamp, dashed ticket seam, saved confirmation, or check-and-tap marker.
- The scroll rhythm uses paper-like layers, staggered deal cards, and ticket rails rather than repetitive centered SaaS sections.
- QR codes are a visible invitation into the TappedIn WhatsApp channel; scan, chat, claim is the secondary access story alongside venue Wi-Fi.
- Campaign scenes should depict multiracial friends and families with an inclusive, candid, South African shopping-day feel.
- The hero device must read as a realistic tall phone, with chat density and UI scaling that preserve a plausible mobile conversation at every breakpoint.
- The scan-chat-claim journey is intentionally legible when skimmed: each story block carries a substantial message, QR, confirmation, or voucher artifact.
- Deal surfaces use notched edges, dashed seams, compact labels, and paper-like overlap; generic rounded white cards are reserved for utility only.
- Turquoise leads every discovery, scan, and claim moment; electric blue remains the dedicated high-attention feature surface.
- The final conversion moment must feel like a deal that has just landed, using a visible QR, voucher, or saved state rather than a generic sign-off.
- Final calls to action point to the scan-to-claim route and use QR, message ticks, check-and-tap, and voucher seams as the signature decorative system.
- Motion is purposeful and infrequent: a scan line, a gentle tap marker float, a deal-arrival nudge, and restrained ticket lifts. All nonessential movement stops when reduced motion is preferred.
- The expanded page uses a **full-bleed deal desk** between the proof rail and journey: a left story column, a layered central voucher deck, and a right chat-status rail. This fills the screen with meaningful product storytelling rather than generic decorative space.
- Desktop composition deliberately uses edge-to-edge side artifacts: scan status, saved-value markers, message timestamps, and ticket rails. On mobile, the same content becomes a linear swipe-like narrative so the page stays legible.
- The reference contributes only high-level cues—clear value-first hierarchy, persistent conversion rhythm, and a dense mix of human imagery with message-like product artifacts. TappedIn retains its own turquoise, QR, WhatsApp, and physical-voucher language.
